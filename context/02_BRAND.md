# PROXI — Brand Guidelines

## Brand Overview

Proxi is a modern technology company that helps people find and hire trusted local professionals.

The brand should communicate:

- Trust
- Professionalism
- Reliability
- Simplicity
- Modern technology
- Human connection

Proxi is **not** just another classifieds platform or directory. It is a trusted marketplace where customers and professionals confidently connect.

---

# Brand Mission

Make hiring trusted professionals as simple and reliable as booking a ride.

---

# Brand Personality

If Proxi were a person, it would be:

- Calm
- Helpful
- Honest
- Professional
- Friendly
- Reliable
- Modern
- Efficient

Never:

- Loud
- Flashy
- Childish
- Aggressive
- Overly corporate
- Overly playful

---

# Brand Voice

All written communication should feel:

Clear

Confident

Helpful

Simple

Positive

Respectful

Professional

Avoid:

- Marketing buzzwords
- Clickbait
- Overpromising
- Technical jargon
- Excessive emojis
- Long paragraphs

Write like a trusted guide, not a salesperson.

---

# Logo

The Proxi logo is the primary visual identifier of the brand.

Official variants:

- Primary Logo
- Dark Logo
- Light Logo
- Favicon

Never:

- Stretch the logo
- Change proportions
- Add effects
- Rotate
- Change colors
- Place on low-contrast backgrounds

Maintain generous clear space around the logo.

# Color Principles

Primary colors should communicate trust.

Avoid highly saturated colors.

Do not use random gradients.

Every color should have a functional purpose.

# Icon Style

Icons should be:

Simple

Rounded

Modern

Consistent stroke width

Examples:

- Lucide
- Phosphor
- Heroicons

Avoid:

- Cartoon icons
- Skeuomorphic icons
- Highly detailed icons
- Mixed icon styles

---

# Illustration Style

Illustrations should feel:

Modern

Friendly

Professional

Minimal

Soft

Human

Characteristics

- Rounded shapes
- Soft shadows
- Limited color palette
- Diverse people
- Positive expressions
- Clean backgrounds

Avoid:

- Comic style
- Anime
- Clipart
- Overly realistic stock photos
- Cheap 3D renders

Illustrations should support the interface rather than dominate it.

---

# Photography

When photographs are used:

Prefer:

- Real professionals
- Real homes
- Natural lighting
- Diverse people
- Authentic environments

Avoid:

- Generic stock photos
- Unrealistic AI images
- Overly posed scenes
- Excessive filters

---

# UI Style

The interface should feel:

Premium

Calm

Clean

Airy

Easy to understand

Use:

- Large spacing
- Rounded corners
- Soft shadows
- Minimal borders
- White space

Avoid:

- Busy layouts
- Dense interfaces
- Excessive animations
- Bright gradients
- Heavy textures

# Shadows

Use subtle shadows only.

The interface should never feel heavy.

Prefer elevation through spacing before adding stronger shadows.

---

# Animations

Animations should be:

Quick

Smooth

Purposeful

Duration

150–250ms

Avoid decorative animations that do not improve usability.

---

# Emotional Experience

Every screen should make users feel:

Safe

Confident

In control

Supported

Never:

Confused

Overwhelmed

Rushed

Distrustful

---

# Accessibility

Maintain sufficient color contrast.

Support keyboard navigation.

Support screen readers.

Do not rely on color alone to communicate meaning.

Touch targets should remain large enough for all users.

# AI Instructions

When generating UI:

- Preserve the Proxi visual identity.
- Reuse approved colors and typography.
- Maintain generous spacing and consistent component styling.
- Do not introduce new colors or visual styles without explicit approval.
- Prioritize clarity and trust over decoration.
- Ensure all assets, illustrations, and layouts reinforce the feeling of a premium technology company.
