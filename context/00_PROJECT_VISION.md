# PROXI — Project Vision

## Overview

Proxi is a trusted local services marketplace that connects customers with verified artisans and skilled professionals.

Instead of relying on referrals, social media posts, or unreliable contacts, customers can quickly discover, compare, book, and pay trusted professionals through one modern platform.

Proxi focuses on trust, transparency, simplicity, and convenience.

The platform launches first in Benin City, Nigeria, before expanding to other cities.

---

# Mission

Make finding trusted local professionals as easy as ordering a ride.

---

# Vision

Become Africa's most trusted marketplace for hiring skilled professionals.

---

# The Problem

Finding reliable artisans is difficult.

Customers often rely on:

- Friends
- Family
- WhatsApp groups
- Facebook posts
- Street referrals

This creates several problems:

- No trust
- No pricing transparency
- No reviews
- No accountability
- Difficult communication
- Poor service quality

Professionals also struggle.

Many skilled artisans depend only on referrals and word-of-mouth, making it difficult to consistently find customers and grow their businesses.

---

# The Solution

Proxi provides a modern marketplace where:

Customers can

- Search professionals
- Compare profiles
- Read reviews
- See transparent pricing
- Book appointments
- Make secure payments
- Track bookings

Professionals can

- Create verified profiles
- Receive nearby job requests
- Manage bookings
- Build reputation
- Grow their business

---

# Core Principles

Every decision made in this project should reinforce these principles.

## Trust First

Trust is the foundation of Proxi.

Every feature should increase user confidence.

Examples:

- Verification
- Reviews
- Ratings
- Identity checks
- Escrow payments
- Professional profiles

---

## Simplicity

The product should always feel easy to use.

Users should never feel overwhelmed.

Interfaces should require as little thinking as possible.

---

## Transparency

Customers should always know:

- Who they are hiring
- What they are paying
- Why they are paying
- What happens next

---

## Local First

Proxi connects users with professionals nearby.

Location is one of the platform's strongest advantages.

---

## Professionalism

The platform should never feel cheap, playful, or untrustworthy.

Visual design should communicate quality and reliability.

# Long-Term Goal

---

# Development Philosophy

This project prioritizes:

- Maintainability
- Scalability
- Security
- Accessibility
- Performance
- Consistency

Quick solutions that introduce technical debt should be avoided whenever practical.

Every feature should be designed with future growth in mind.

---

# AI Instructions

When implementing features:

- Always prioritize user trust.
- Keep interfaces simple.
- Reuse existing components whenever possible.
- Avoid unnecessary complexity.
- Maintain consistency with the design system.
- Prefer scalable architecture over quick fixes.
- Do not introduce features outside the approved project scope without explicit instruction.
